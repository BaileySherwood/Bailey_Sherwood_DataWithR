Part 2 06/08/2026

Gestalt

Comments

Objective:
The objective of the image is to show that SpaceX overwhelmingly owns more operational satellites than any other organisation.

Preattentive Processing:
The first thing that grabbed my attention was the size of the SpaceX rocket, showing that SpaceX has launched substantially more satellites than any other organisation.

Gestalt Law:
The infographic uses the Gestalt Law of Proximity by placing all non-SpaceX satellite owners close together beside one rocket. This causes viewers to perceive them as a single comparison group, while SpaceX is isolated to emphasise its significantly larger satellite fleet.

Visual Comparison Accuracy:
The image mainly uses length to compare satellite ownership, with the height of the rocket representing the number of satellites.

Reference:
Visual Capitalist. (2026). Ranked: Who owns the most satellites? Voronoi.

Module 3 24/08/2026

The objective of this task was to gather source information and check the validity of data presented online against factual statistics in order to determine how robust the dataset was. The chart compares the deadliest pandemics by the percentage of the global population they killed, showing that the Black Death had the greatest proportional effect on the population. The question, data and visuals work together, but the term “deadliest” may be misleading because the pandemics are ranked by population percentage rather than total deaths. The graphic’s colours may also reduce clarity because they do not present a clear visual trend.

Reference:
Ang, C. (2020). 5 visualizations we wish we had published in March 2020. HowMuch.net. View the source
